# E-commerce Refund & Return Policy

## Refund eligibility
Orders that have not yet shipped can be cancelled for a full refund. Once an order has
shipped, customers may initiate a return within 24 days of delivery for a full refund,
provided the item is unused and in its original packaging.

## Refund timeline
Once a cancellation or return is processed, refunds are issued to the original payment
method within 5-7 business days.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or is not what was ordered, report it within 48 hours of
delivery for an expedited replacement or full refund, with no return shipping cost charged
to the customer.

## Non-refundable items
Gift cards, digital downloads, and clearance items marked "final sale" are not eligible
for return or refund.
