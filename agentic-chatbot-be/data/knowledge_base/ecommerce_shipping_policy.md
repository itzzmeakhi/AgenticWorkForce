# Shipping & Tracking Policy

## Delivery timelines
Standard shipping within India takes 3-5 business days from dispatch. Expedited shipping
(where available) takes 1-2 business days for an additional fee.

## Tracking
A tracking number and carrier are assigned once an order moves to "Shipped" status. Use the
track-package tool with the order ID to get the current location and estimated delivery date.

## Delayed or lost packages
If a package shows no movement for more than 3 business days, or the estimated delivery
date has passed by more than 2 days, escalate to a human agent for carrier investigation.
