# Insurance Claims Process

## How to file a claim
A claim can be filed on any active policy. Tell the support agent which policy it is for,
what happened, the type of claim (for example hospitalisation or accident damage) and the
amount you are claiming. You receive a claim number straight away and the claim starts in
the "submitted" status. Claims cannot be filed on lapsed or expired policies.

## Claim statuses
A claim moves through four statuses: submitted (received), under review (documents and bills
are being verified), approved (a payout amount has been decided) or rejected (not covered
or documents insufficient). The decision note on the claim explains the outcome.

## Documents required
Health claims need the hospital bill, discharge summary and doctor's prescription. Motor
claims need photos of the damage, the repair estimate and a copy of the registration
certificate. Home claims need photos, an itemised list of losses and, for theft or fire, a
police or fire-brigade report. Missing documents are the most common cause of delay.

## Claim timeline
Most claims are reviewed within 7 working days of receiving complete documents. Approved
claims are paid to the registered bank account within 5 working days of approval. Complex
claims such as large hospitalisations or accident cases that need a surveyor can take up to
21 days.

## Rejected claims and appeals
If a claim is rejected the decision note gives the reason. You can appeal within 30 days by
sending additional documents; an appeal is reviewed by a different claims officer.
