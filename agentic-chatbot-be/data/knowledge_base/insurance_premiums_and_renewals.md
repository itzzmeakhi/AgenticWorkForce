# Insurance Premiums & Renewals

## Paying your premium
Premiums are paid once a year on or before the due date shown on the policy. Payment can be
made by UPI, net banking, debit or credit card. The next premium due date and amount are
shown when you ask for your policy details.

## Grace period
If a premium is missed, health and home policies have a 30-day grace period and motor
policies a 15-day grace period. Cover continues during the grace period, but claims made for
events after the grace period ends are not payable.

## Lapsed policies
A policy lapses when the premium is not paid by the end of the grace period. A lapsed policy
provides no cover and no new claim can be filed on it. A lapsed policy can be reinstated
within 90 days by paying the overdue premium; after that a fresh policy must be taken and
waiting periods start again.

## Renewal
Policies renew for a further year when the premium is paid. Renewal reminders are sent 30
days before the end date. A no-claim bonus of up to 20 percent on motor policies and 10
percent on the sum insured for health policies applies when no claim was made in the
previous year.

## Premium changes
Premiums can change at renewal depending on age, claim history and the sum insured chosen.
Increasing the sum insured at renewal is possible; reducing it takes effect from the next
policy year.
