# General Contact Center FAQ

## How do I sign in?
Use the Sign in button and choose the account type: E-commerce for orders and deliveries,
or Insurance for policies and claims. They are separate accounts, so sign in to the one
that matches your question. Questions about products and our policies need no sign-in.

## How do I reach a human agent?
Ask to "speak to a human" or "escalate" at any point in the conversation, and the agent will
note the request; live handoff to a person is planned for a future milestone.

## What are your support hours?
This assistant is available 24/7, by text chat and, where voice is enabled, by voice using
the microphone and speaker buttons next to the message box.

## Do you support insurance questions?
Yes. After signing in to your insurance account you can ask about your policies, their
premiums and renewal dates, your claims and their status, and you can file a new claim.
General questions about how claims, premiums and coverage work need no sign-in.
