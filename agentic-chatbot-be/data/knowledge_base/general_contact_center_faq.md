# General Contact Center FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password", enter your email, and check your inbox
for a reset link (arrives within 5 minutes; check spam if not). The link is valid for 24
hours. New passwords must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and a number.

## How do I reach a human agent?
Ask to "speak to a human" or "escalate" at any point in the conversation, and the agent will
note the request; live handoff is planned for a future milestone (see README roadmap).

## What are your support hours?
This assistant is available 24/7 for text chat in this build. Voice support is a roadmap
item for the full build (see README "What's WIP").

## Do you support insurance questions yet?
Insurance domain routing works (the agent correctly recognizes an insurance question), but
policy and claim lookups are not implemented yet in this milestone -- that ships in the full
build. Ask about an order or product for a fully working example in the meantime.
