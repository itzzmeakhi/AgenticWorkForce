# Insurance Coverage & Exclusions

## Health insurance coverage
Health plans cover hospitalisation of 24 hours or more, day-care procedures, ambulance
charges and pre and post hospitalisation expenses for 30 and 60 days. The sum insured is the
maximum payable in a policy year. Family floater plans share one sum insured between all
covered members.

## Motor insurance coverage
Comprehensive car and two-wheeler cover pays for accidental damage to your vehicle, theft,
fire, natural calamities and third-party liability. Third-party-only cover pays for injury or
damage to other people and property, but not for damage to your own vehicle. A deductible
is deducted from every own-damage claim.

## Home insurance coverage
Home plans cover the building structure and contents against fire, flood, storm, burglary
and accidental damage up to the sum insured. Wear and tear and gradual deterioration are
not covered.

## Waiting periods
New health policies have a 30-day initial waiting period, except for accidents. Pre-existing
conditions are covered after 24 months of continuous cover. Specific procedures such as
joint replacement have a 24-month waiting period.

## Common exclusions
Cosmetic and dental treatment (unless caused by an accident), self-inflicted injury, injury
while driving under the influence, war and nuclear risks, and any loss caused by
deliberately misleading information given when buying the policy are not covered.
